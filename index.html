    Your auction budget

    Starting balance10,000JC
    • Same budget for every teamEvery team starts with the same balance of JIDO Credits (JC). Credits cannot be added, traded or shared.
    • You bid inside your hallEach hall holds a fixed number of teams and runs its own auction of the same components. When a hall is full, new teams are asked to move to the next hall. You compete only against teams in your hall.
    • Components are sold in slotsEach slot opens about 6 components together on one shared clock. Bid on any of them. Teams can see every bid live.
    • Raise by at least 2 JCYour first bid must be at least the starting bid. Each raise must beat your own last bid by 2 JC or more. Bids cannot be lowered or withdrawn.
    • Late bids extend the clockA bid in the last 10 seconds adds 10 seconds, so nobody can win by sniping.
    • Plan your balanceThe bids you are currently winning must fit within your balance. You cannot bid more than you can pay.
    • Highest bids winIf 3 lots are available, the 3 highest bids when the clock ends win a lot each and pay their own final bid. Equal bids go to the earlier bid.
    • The shop is always openBasic items such as wiring, tape and breadboards are sold at a fixed price, first come first served, until stock runs out.
    • Only your parts countYou can build only with components you won or bought. Credits left after the auction earn bonus points.
